SYMPTOMS

A. Early symptoms

  • Memory loss affecting daily life(forgetting names,etc)
  • Difficulty planning or solving problems.
  • Trouble completing familiar tass.
  • Confusion with time and place.
  • Misplacing things and struggling to retrace steps.
  • Changes in mood and personality(depression,anxiety).

B. Middle-stage symptoms

  • Increased memory loss and confusion.
  • Difficulty recognizing friends and family.
  • Repetitive speech and difficulty communicating.
  • Wandering and getting lost.
  • Sleep disturbances.

C.Late-stage symptoms

  • Severe memory loss and inability to recognize loved ones.
  • Loss of ability to communicate.
  • Difficulty swallowing and mobility issues.
  • Need for full-time assistance with daily activities.
